# Firewood Shelter

TODO: Update with parameters from model

A freestanding outdoor firewood shelter sized to hold a full cord of split wood. Three H4 treated sleepers form the skid foundation, kept in place by driven rebar pegs so no concrete is needed. Six H4 posts carry a skillion (single-slope) corrugated iron roof that drains to the rear. The front face is fully open for easy stacking and retrieval. The back wall is clad with vertical rough-sawn paling for weather protection and airflow.

## Status

**ready-to-build**

## Dimensions

- Overall footprint: 2600 mm W × 2400 mm D
- Front wall height (top of roof framing): 1965 mm
- Back wall height (top of roof framing): 1565 mm
- Roof slope: ~9.5° skillion (front high, back low)
- Roof overhang: 300 mm front, 150 mm rear, 150 mm each side

## Materials

- Foundation: 3 × H4 treated sleepers 200 × 50 × 2400 mm
- Posts: 6 × H4 treated 100 × 100 (3 front @ 1800 mm, 3 back @ 1400 mm above sleeper)
- Framing: 90 × 45 CCA treated pine — wall plates, 5 rafters, 4 back-wall noggins
- Back wall: 28 × rough-sawn paling 100 × 18 × 1500 mm (vertical)
- Roof: 4 × corrugated iron sheet 810 × 3300 mm
- Fixings: 75 × 75 galv angle brackets, roofing screws, joist hanger nails, coach screws
- Anchoring: 8 × 12 mm rebar pegs 1200 mm long (400 mm proud of ground)

## Joinery

- Posts toe-nailed and angle-bracketed to sleepers (no mortise required)
- Wall plates face-screwed and angle-bracketed on top of posts
- Rafters angle-bracketed to wall plates at each end; no birdsmouth cut
- Back-wall noggins face-nailed between posts at 1/3 and 2/3 height
- Paling boards butt-jointed vertically, nailed to noggins and top plate

## Notes

- Sleeper spacing: 1200 mm centres (3 sleepers = 2400 mm total width)
- Rebar pegs driven through sleepers at front, rear, and mid-sleeper ends to resist fore-aft and lateral movement
- Board count for back wall: 26 boards needed across 2600 mm span; 28 purchased (2 spare)
- Rafters pitched at matching slope — all five identical, no compound cuts
- Budget total: ~$630
