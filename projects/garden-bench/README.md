# Garden Bench

A 1200 mm two-seater outdoor bench with a slatted seat and slatted back, built from cedar. The structural frame uses through-tenon joints with wedges — each seat rail tenon passes completely through its leg and is locked with a hardwood wedge driven into a saw kerf in the tenon end. This is the same end-frame structure as a dining table, at a manageable scale.

## Status
**planning**

## Dimensions
- Overall: 1200 W × 380 D × 450 mm seat height
- Back height above seat: ~380 mm
- Leg section: 70 × 70 mm
- Seat rail: 70 × 35 mm
- Slats: 70 × 28 mm (4 seat, 2 back)

## Materials
- Primary: cedar 70 × 70 mm (legs/back posts), cedar DAR (rails and slats)
- Fasteners: stainless 50 mm screws (slats to rails), hardwood wedges (tenons)
- Adhesive: exterior PVA

## Joinery
- **Through mortise and tenon with wedges** — seat rails pass through each leg; tenon ends are kerf-sawn and wedged for a permanent mechanical lock; no screws in the structural frame
- **Slat attachment** — seat and back slats screwed to rails with plugged stainless screws (or gap-spaced and left slightly proud)

## Notes
- Through-wedged tenons are ideal for outdoor furniture: they can be re-wedged if seasonal movement loosens them, and they look handsome as a design feature
- The front legs extend above seat height to form back posts — single piece of stock, no joint at that transition
- This bench replicates one end-frame of the target dining table; build two and you have the confidence to scale up
