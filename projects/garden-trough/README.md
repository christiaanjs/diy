# Garden Trough

A simple rectangular outdoor planter built from dressed cedar. The base panel sits in a dado groove routed (or sawn-and-chiselled) into the side walls, and the corners are rabbet-jointed. This is a deliberate skills project: the dado and rabbet joints introduced here are the two foundation joints for all box and carcass construction, and will reappear in every furniture project that follows.

## Status
**planning**

## Dimensions
- Overall: 800 × 300 × 300 mm (L × W × H)
- Wall thickness: 19 mm dressed cedar
- Base panel: 762 × 262 × 19 mm (sits in 6 mm dado, 30 mm up from bottom)

## Materials
- Primary: 90 × 19 mm cedar DAR (dressed all round)
- Fasteners: stainless 40 mm screws (exterior grade)
- Adhesive: exterior-grade PVA

## Joinery
- **Dado joint** — 6 mm deep groove in each side wall, base panel drops in; cut with two saw passes + chisel, or router
- **Rabbet joint** — end boards lap over the ends of the side boards at each corner

## Notes
- Drainage holes (4 × 2 grid, 20 mm dia) drilled in base before assembly
- Seal all end grain with oil before assembly; end grain wicks moisture fast in outdoor use
- No finish required on cedar — it weathers to grey naturally; or apply timber oil to keep colour
- Stainless or hot-dipped galvanised fasteners only outdoors — zinc plated rust quickly
