# Outdoor Dining Set

An outdoor dining table and two matching benches in hardwood (iroko, kwila, or similar durable species). The table seats 6–8; each bench seats 3–4. All structural joints are mortise and tenon; tops are edge-joined solid panels with breadboard ends. This is the capstone project of the learning progression.

## Status
**planning**

## Dimensions
- Table: 1800 W × 900 D × 750 H mm
- Bench (× 2): 1600 W × 350 D × 450 H mm
- Table legs: 90 × 90 mm
- Bench legs: 50 × 50 mm
- Aprons: 90 × 40 mm
- Top / seat panels: ~22–40 mm solid timber

## Materials
- Primary: hardwood (iroko, kwila, or treated pine as budget alternative)
- Fasteners: stainless steel throughout; wooden buttons for top fixing
- Adhesive: exterior PVA

## Joinery
- **Mortise and tenon** — all leg-to-apron joints; tenons 15 mm thick × 55 mm tall
- **Edge joint** — tabletop from 5 × 200 mm boards glued up to ~900 mm width
- **Breadboard ends** — cross-grain end caps glued at centre only, slotted at edges to allow seasonal panel movement
- **Wooden button / groove** — top fixed to apron frame to allow ±5 mm movement without splitting

## Notes
- Hardwood choice matters outdoors: iroko and kwila have natural oil content that resists moisture; avoid untreated pine which will rot in 3–5 years
- At 900 mm width, the table panel glue-up requires 4 pipe clamps minimum; cauls are essential to prevent bowing
- Build the two benches first — they use the same joinery at a smaller, more forgiving scale
- Oil finish (not varnish) is preferred outdoors: when it weathers you simply reapply, whereas varnish must be stripped before refinishing
- Budget ~$1,300 in materials; hardwood species and source will shift this significantly
